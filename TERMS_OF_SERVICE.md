What Career Autopilot is.** A placement-readiness platform: students build and verify proof-of-work (projects, resumes, skills); college placement cells that students consent to share with get cohort-readiness visibility. It provides estimates and tooling — not a placement agency, recruiter, examination body, or law/IP firm.
