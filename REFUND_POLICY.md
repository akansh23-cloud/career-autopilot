# Refund & Cancellation Policy — Career Autopilot
**Effective:** 12 July 2026 · Canonical in-app version: `#/legal/refunds`.

**Free tier and pilots.** The core student experience and college pilots are free — nothing to refund.

**Cancellation.** Cancel any time from Settings; access runs to the end of the paid period and simply doesn't renew. No cancellation fee.

**Refunds.**
- Duplicate charge, failed activation, or payment captured without the plan unlocking: **full refund** — email support with the payment id.
- **First purchase** of a plan: 7-day money-back window for good-faith usage.
- Renewals and partial periods are otherwise non-refundable, except where law requires.
- Approved refunds go to the original payment method within **5–7 business days** of approval (bank timelines may add days).

**College contracts.** Institutional agreements follow the signed order form's refund/exit clauses; absent those, the consumer terms above apply pro-rata.

**Contact.** Support and grievance addresses are served live at `GET /api/legal` and the in-app Contact tab.
