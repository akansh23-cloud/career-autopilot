# Privacy Policy — Career Autopilot
**Effective:** 12 July 2026 · **Consent version:** `2026-07-dpdp-v1`

Career Autopilot is a placement-readiness platform for engineering students and their college placement cells. This policy is written to be read, and it maps to India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP) and its Rules. Where this document and the in-app consent screen differ, the in-app screen you actually accepted governs.

## 1. What we collect, and why

| Data | Why we need it | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Name, email, profile photo | Account identity and sign-in | Your Google account or dev sign-in |
| Profile (branch, batch, skills, target roles) | Computing your readiness and matching guidance | You |
| Resumes, resume scores and version history | The resume engine's core job | You upload; scores are computed deterministically |
| Projects, GitHub links, verification evidence, viva answers, skill XP | Verified proof-of-work — the point of the platform | You submit; verification is rule-based |
| Patent-workspace drafts, prior-art plans | The Innovation/Patent OS features you use | You |
| Activity events (logins, submissions) | Momentum, streaks, at-risk detection | Generated by your use |
| College membership (which college, how you joined) | Scoping your data to the right placement cell | Join code, roster match, or email-domain match |
| Payment status (plan, order ids) | Access control for paid features | Payment provider; we never store card numbers |

We do **not** collect data from your device beyond what the app needs, we do not buy data about you, and we show no ads.

## 2. Who can see what

- **You** see everything about you, always.
- **Your college's placement cell** sees your readiness score, resume score, verified projects, skill coverage and activity signals — **only if** (a) your account is linked to that college **and** (b) you accepted the college-visibility consent. Either can be undone at any time (Settings → My College → Leave, or the consent toggle). The server enforces this filter; it is not cosmetic.
- **Recruiters** see nothing unless you explicitly publish a recruiter-visible profile — a separate opt-in, off by default, with field-level consent shaping.
- **Platform admins** can access account records for support, verification approvals and abuse handling, logged and minimal.
- **Nobody else.** We do not sell or share personal data with third parties for their own purposes.

## 3. Your rights (built into the product, not a mailbox)

- **Access / portability:** Settings → *Export my data* (`GET /api/account/export`) returns a complete JSON copy across every collection we hold on you — the export iterates the same source list the deletion cascade uses, so it cannot silently miss a collection.
- **Correction:** edit your profile, resumes and projects directly.
- **Erasure:** *Delete my account* (`DELETE /api/account`) schedules deletion with a **7-day grace window**; signing back in within the window cancels it. After the window, a cascade permanently removes your data from every collection, roster entries under your email, and your task assignments.
- **Withdraw consent:** the college-visibility toggle and Leave College act immediately; withdrawing does not degrade the rest of your account.
- **Grievance:** support tickets in-app, or the grievance contact published on the deployment's about page. We respond within the timelines the DPDP Rules require.

## 4. Consent, minors, and colleges

- Consent is **explicit and versioned**: on first sign-in (and whenever this policy materially changes) the app blocks feature use until you accept the current version; your acceptance and choices are recorded with a timestamp.
- Some first-year students are under 18. Where the account holder is a minor, DPDP requires verifiable parental/guardian consent; colleges onboarding minors through the roster flow are responsible for collecting it as the institution facilitating the service, and we provide the data-handling one-pager (`docs/DPDP_DATA_HANDLING.md`) to support that process.
- When a college imports a roster, we store the roster rows (name, email, branch, batch, roll no) as **invitations**. A roster row becomes an active account only when that student signs in and accepts consent themselves.

## 5. Storage and security

Data lives in a MongoDB deployment configured by the operator (for Indian colleges we recommend and default to a Mumbai region cluster). In transit everything is TLS. Passwords are never stored (OAuth only). Sessions are httpOnly, signed, SameSite cookies. Access is role-scoped and college-scoped server-side (RBAC + tenant filters), rate-limited, and every response carries a request id tied to a 30-day error log for accountability. Verified-credential signing uses Ed25519 keys with rotation support.

## 6. Retention

- Account data: kept while your account exists; erased by the deletion cascade after the 7-day grace window.
- Error logs: auto-expire after 30 days. Usage counters: 3 days.
- Roster invitations: kept while the college workspace is active; removed by the cascade when the matching account is deleted.

## 7. Changes

Material changes bump the consent version, which re-triggers the in-app consent screen — you will never be governed by a version you did not accept.
